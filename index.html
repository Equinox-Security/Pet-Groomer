Hi, I’m Kim, the founder and lead stylist at Shear Pawfection Pet Grooming. I specialize in breed-specific haircuts, creative styling, and skin-soothing therapies. My ultimate goal is to provide a calm, positive environment where your furry friends can feel safe, pampered, and loved. When I’m not at the styling table, I’m volunteering at local shelters or hiking with my golden retriever, Barnaby.
